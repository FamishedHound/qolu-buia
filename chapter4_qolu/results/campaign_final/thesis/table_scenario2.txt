Conversions: parcels converted in the run; one decimal; no best value is marked.
C_align x |P| / Conversions, per run in this table (|P| = 2500 parcels): 0.991 to 1.001.
  Every conversion here turns a whole non-urban parcel urban, which moves the land-type distribution of each adjacent parcel j by exactly 1/n_j whatever the land types were, so C_align is close to Conversions / |P| and differences in C_align follow differences in the number of conversions.
Bold: the best mean at full precision in the column (highest for Land Use, Woodland and Urban; lowest for C_align); none for Conversions.
